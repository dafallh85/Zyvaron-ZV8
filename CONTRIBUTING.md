# Contributing to Zyvaron

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Zyvaron.

Zyvaron explores the convergence of Artificial Intelligence, blockchain technology, data intelligence, automation, and Web3.

## Ways to Contribute

Contributions may include:

- Documentation
- Technical improvements
- Development ideas
- AI research
- Web3 research
- Developer tools
- Bug reports
- Security reports
- Community suggestions

## Guidelines

Contributions should be:

- Clear
- Accurate
- Relevant
- Respectful
- Technically responsible

Do not submit private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or other sensitive information.

## Pull Requests

Before submitting a pull request:

1. Clearly describe the proposed change.
2. Explain its purpose.
3. Keep changes focused.
4. Check technical information for accuracy.
5. Avoid unrelated changes.

## Issues

When reporting an issue, include:

- A clear description
- Steps to reproduce when applicable
- Expected behavior
- Actual behavior
- Relevant technical information

## Security

For security vulnerabilities, follow the instructions in `SECURITY.md`.

Do not publicly disclose sensitive vulnerability details before appropriate review.

## Disclaimer

Contributions to this repository do not constitute financial, investment, trading, legal, or professional advice.

Zyvaron reserves the right to review, modify, reject, or remove contributions that do not align with the project's goals.
